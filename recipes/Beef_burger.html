Beef Burger

Description

A beef burger is a popular meal made with a cooked beef patty placed inside a soft burger bun. It is served with fresh ingredients such as lettuce, tomato and onion, along with cheese and sauces. BBQ sauce adds a smoky and slightly sweet flavour that makes the burger even more delicious.

Ingredients

  • Beef patties
  • Burger buns
  • Cheese
  • Lettuce
  • Tomato
  • Onion
  • Tomato sauce
  • BBQ sauce
  • Mayonnaise

Steps

  1. Cut the burger bun in half.
  2. Prepare the lettuce, tomato and onion by washing and slicing them.
  3. Heat a little cooking oil in a pan.
  4. Cook the beef patty on both sides until fully cooked.
  5. Place a slice of cheese on the hot beef patty and allow it to melt.
  6. Lightly toast the burger buns in a pan.
  7. Spread BBQ sauce on the bottom half of the burger bun.
  8. Place the lettuce, tomato and onion on the bun.
  9. Add the cooked beef patty with melted cheese.
  10. Cover with the top half of the bun
