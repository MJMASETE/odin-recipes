Kota

Description

A Kota is a popular South African street food made using a quarter loaf of bread that is hollowed out and filled with delicious ingredients.

Ingredients

  • quarter loaf of fresh white bread
  • 3 potatoes
  • 2 slices of polony
  • 1 fried egg
  • 1 slice of cheese
  • Russian sausage
  • Atchar
  • Tomato sauce
  • BBQ sauce
  • Salt
  • Seasoning
  • Cooking oil

Steps

  1. Prepare the chips: Peel and cut the potato into thin chips. Rinse and dry them.
  2. Fry the chips: Heat cooking oil in a pan and fry the chips until golden and crispy. Add a little salt and Seasoning
  3. Prepare the polony: Fry the polony slices lightly in a pan
  4. Fry the egg
  5. Fry the Russian
  6. Prepare the bread: Cut a quarter loaf of bread and carefully hollow out the inside
  7. Add the fillings: Spread atchar and add the fried polony with Chips inside the hollow part of the bread
  8. place the cheese slice on top of the hot chips
  9. add the Russian on top of the cheese with the egg
  10. Close the kota with the piece of bread you removed when hollowing the quarter bread to complete it
